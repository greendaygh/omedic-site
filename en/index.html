Signature

Cosmetic Facial Acupuncture

Our Jeong-an-chim program is designed to support a clearer, livelier complexion. It aims to improve facial blood and lymphatic circulation and stimulate natural collagen production to help restore elasticity and hydration.

  • • Circulation support for complexion and overall condition
  • • Collagen-stimulating care for elasticity and hydration
  • • Release of tense facial muscles and asymmetry care

Recommended cycle: Once a week · 5–10 sessions recommended

Signature

Skin Care (V-Lift)

We diagnose the underlying causes of skin concerns such as acne, sensitivity, and pigmentation, then combine energy-based devices with bio-venom boosters to help improve texture and elasticity.

  • • Hybrid lifting with Volnewmer & Shurink
  • • Bio-Venom skin booster
  • • Premium facial thread lift & wild-ginseng contouring

Recommended cycle: Once a week · 4–8 weeks of follow-up

Focused Care

Hair Loss Care

Rather than simple topical application, we deliver concentrated hair-root boosters into micro-channels created by the Purecel laser to help support a healthier scalp environment for hair growth.

  • • Precise scalp micro-channeling
  • • Direct delivery of hair-growth factors and bio-actives
  • • Personalized herbal care to calm scalp heat

Recommended cycle: Every 2 weeks · 3-month care cycle

Everyday Recovery

Pain Management

We analyze the root causes of back, shoulder, and joint pain to help restore everyday movement and comfort.

  • • Muscle tension and circulation recovery
  • • Posture and body-pattern review
  • • Personalized plans for acute and chronic pain

Recommended cycle: 2–3 times per week · tailored to symptoms

Personalized

Immunity Care

We support constitutional balance to help reduce fatigue build-up and recurring minor illnesses.

  • • Constitution-based tonic plan
  • • Seasonal immune-support care
  • • Herbal prescriptions aimed at improving recovery

Recommended cycle: 1–2 visits per month for ongoing care
